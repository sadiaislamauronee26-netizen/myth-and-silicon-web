“

শেক্সপিয়ারের হ্যামলেট শুধু প্রতিশোধের গল্প নয়, মানুষের মনের গভীর দ্বিধাদ্বন্দ্ব আর কষ্টের এক নিখুঁত ছবি। এই প্রজেক্টের ছবি ও ভিডিওতে দুর্গের ঠান্ডা পরিবেশ এবং রাতের কুয়াশা দিয়ে হ্যামলেটের ভেতরের অনিশ্চয়তা ও সিদ্ধান্তহীনতাকে ফুটিয়ে তোলা হয়েছে। এআই প্রযুক্তির সাহায্যে সাহিত্যের সেই পুরোনো একাকীত্বকে জীবন্ত করে তোলাই আমার মূল লক্ষ্য।

"Shakespeare’s Hamlet is more than just a revenge story; it shows the deep conflict and pain of the human mind. In this project, the cold castle and dark mist reflect Hamlet’s loneliness and doubt. My goal is to use AI to bring this classic literary story to life."

CURATED DRAMA SERIES

The Hamlet Series

Sequential visual breakdown of key dramatic acts

ACT I • THE WEIGHT OF EXISTENCE

Theme: Melancholia & Existential Paralysis

Solitary contemplation upon the freezing ramparts of Elsinore. The cold chiaroscuro lighting and drifting fog visually capture Hamlet’s profound indecision and mental burden before the specter of truth.

ACT I • SHADOWS OF VENGEANCE

Theme: Moral Ambiguity & Supernatural Dread

The spectral encounter with King Hamlet's ghost in the misty courtyard. High contrast shadows and ethereal haze embody the fragile boundary between justice and destructive madness.

ACT IV • DROWNED IN SORROW

Theme: Fragility, Grief & Lost Innocence

Ophelia drifting down the weeping brook, surrounded by floating wild petals. Soft, melancholic water reflections mirror the tragic surrender of innocence to an unyielding reality.

ACT V • MEDITATION ON DUST

Theme: Mortality & The Absurdity of Power

Holding Yorick’s skull in the windswept graveyard. The earthy, muted palette strips away all regal vanity, reflecting Hamlet’s ultimate realization of life’s fleeting absurdity.

BEHIND THE SCENES

The Creative & Technical Pipeline

How Shakespearean dramaturgy was translated into an interactive web exhibition

◆

Generative Direction

  • Leonardo AI: High-fidelity concept rendering, chiaroscuro lighting, and gothic costume consistency.
  • MiniMax: Atmospheric motion loops, subtle sea mist physics, and cinematic camera panning.
  • Prompt Architecture: Iterative negative prompting, tonal control, and classical oil painting textures.
◆

Cognitive & Dev Engine

  • Gemini: Dramaturgical co-pilot, literary scene breakdown, and semantic frontend scaffolding.
  • Google Flow: Multimodal asset orchestration and creative workflow structuring.
  • Frontend Toolchain: Semantic HTML5, dark luxury CSS3, Vanilla ES6 JavaScript, and Antigravity IDE.
